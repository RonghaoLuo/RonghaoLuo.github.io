Unity · Arcade prototype

Space Shooter

A top-down arcade shooter where you pilot a ship through an asteroid arena, collect weapons and power-ups, and take on more enemies as your score climbs.

I refactored the enemy classes, built chain reactions for explosive enemies, and added sound and visual effects to make combat more responsive. I also built an all-weapons mode for testing and free play.
